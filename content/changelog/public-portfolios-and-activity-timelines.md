Two surfaces landed for the people BuilderHunt indexes, not just the people
searching it.

**Portfolios.** If you have claimed and verified your builder profile, you get a
public portfolio page you control: the work you want shown, in the order you
want it, with your own topics, bio and open-to status. Verification is bound to
the source it came from, so a claim proves control of *that* account rather than
a general assertion of identity — and platform admins can revoke a claim that
turns out to be wrong.

**Timelines.** Every builder profile now has a public activity timeline that
merges what the sources actually reported — commits, posts, packages, threads —
in date order, with a link to each original. No screenshots, no reconstruction,
no scoring: it exists so that "this person is active" is a claim you can check
in ten seconds instead of taking on faith from a badge.

Both pages are indexable and both respect processing restrictions: a subject who
asks not to be processed is removed across every organization's view, not just
the one that asked.
