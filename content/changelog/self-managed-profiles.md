title: Write your own profile, no GitHub account required

BuilderHunt indexes public developer activity. If you have not published code,
questions, articles, packages or models on any of the platforms we read, there
was nothing for us to show — and no way for you to be found here at all. That was
true of translators, technical writers, illustrators, researchers, consultants,
community managers, and anyone whose work simply does not leave a trace in a
connector's index.

Now you can write your own page. Bio, headline, languages, the services you
offer, the topics you work in, and up to twelve work samples — PDFs, images,
audio or video, 25 MB each. It lives at `/u/your-handle`, and you decide whether
it is public, reachable only by link, or a draft nobody but you can see.

**It is marked `Self-managed` everywhere it appears, and it never carries the
verified badge.** That is not a limitation we are apologising for; it is the
point. A verified badge on BuilderHunt means somebody proved they control an
external account, and a page you wrote about yourself has proved nothing. Both
kinds of profile appear in the same searches, side by side, each labelled for
what it is — so a reader knows in one glance which is which, without reading a
page of caveats.

Attachments are scanned before anyone can open them. An upload is checked against
its own bytes — hash, real length, magic bytes — then quarantined until an
antivirus pass clears it. Nothing is served from a state other than clean, and if
the scanner cannot answer, the file stays where it is: "we ran out of chances to
check" is not a verdict.

If you later prove an external account through the normal claim flow, you can
link it to your page. Your words, your handle and your work samples stay exactly
where they are and the verified block is added — and you can unlink it again just
as easily. Similarity never does this on your behalf: a matching handle or an
identical display name links nothing, ever.

You can turn yourself off from other people's matching surfaces at any time in
your preferences, and it changes what lists contain and nothing else. Your
profile is in your data export, and deleting your account takes it — the page,
the files, and its entry in search — with it.
