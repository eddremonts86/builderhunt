title: Team fit — how a candidate complements the team you already have

Sourcing tools rank candidates against a query. Nobody hires against a query;
they hire against a team that already exists and has gaps.

Team fit analysis compares a candidate's public signals against the builders your
organization already tracks and reports where they overlap and where they extend
the group: languages nobody on the list works in, ecosystems already three-deep,
activity patterns that complement or duplicate. The output is a comparison, not a
score with a decimal point — a "72% culture match" number would be inventing
precision we do not have.

It runs on the shared AI platform, so it is credit-metered, cached per
organization, and degrades to the parts that need no model when the provider is
unavailable.
