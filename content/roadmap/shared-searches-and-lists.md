Teams, roles, seats and organization-scoped storage all exist, and every private table is keyed to an organization. What is missing is the sharing itself: a saved search or a builder list that belongs to the team rather than to whoever created it, with per-resource visibility. This is the dependency the team activity feed is waiting on.
