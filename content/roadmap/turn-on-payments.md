Everything behind checkout is built and tested: Stripe Checkout, the customer portal, signed webhooks with an encrypted durable inbox, the credit ledger with reservations, annual grants, dunning, refunds, disputes, daily reconciliation and an accounting export. What is left is operational, not code — sandbox and Test Clock certification, and a small first-country canary. Until those close, payments stay off and paid tiers are granted manually.
