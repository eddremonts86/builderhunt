Push a list or a sprint's candidates into the ATS your team already runs, deduplicated, with status flowing back — contacted, interviewing, hired. Right now the cycle dies outside the product: BuilderHunt helps you find and contact people, then loses the thread. This is also the only way we get real outcome data to check whether our scoring predicts anything.
