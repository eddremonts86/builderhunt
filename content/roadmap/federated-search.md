One query, fifteen source adapters — GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, SourceHut, Hacker News, Reddit, DEV.to, Hashnode, Stack Overflow, npm, Hugging Face, Lobsters, Devpost, Product Hunt and Bluesky — deduplicated across sources, scored for recency, and cached. Sources are selected per query, so a narrow search stays fast.
