From a builder's profile, see their frequent co-authors on commits, the people they are co-mentioned with in threads, and who they launch alongside — click any node to open that profile, filter by link strength, time window and source. It is discovery through the network instead of through a query: you need a frontend engineer, X does frontend, but the person who ships best with X is Y. Needs one new edge table and a background worker fed by co-author data.
