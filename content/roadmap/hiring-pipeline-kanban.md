Customizable columns over the builders you already track (New → Reviewed → Contacted → In conversation → Hired), with a timestamp and notes per stage and filters by stage or owner inside your organization. Today BuilderHunt helps you discover and track; the moment you are talking to five people at once, that state lives in a CSV export and some loose notes. This is the step from sourcing tool to hiring tool.
