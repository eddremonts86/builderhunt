Send an invitation from a builder's profile; the candidate picks a slot in a portal with no account and no password. Booking, cancelling and rescheduling are atomic under an advisory lock. Alongside it, a calendar that merges interviews, worker runs and alert deliveries, with timezone-correct recurrence, a private ICS export and verified reminder delivery.
