An AI review of a candidate's public work — what it does, what it suggests about how they work, and what to ask them — and a comparison of a candidate against the builders your organization already tracks. Both produce structured, schema-validated output, both are credit-metered, and neither returns a hire/no-hire verdict: that is an explicit prohibited output, not an oversight.
