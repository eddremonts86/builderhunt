Per saved search: how many of its results you actually engaged with in the last 30 days, how long since it last produced a useful match, how many alerts fired versus how many you opened, and a plain recommendation — kill it, adjust the query, or leave it alone. Once you have dozens of searches and alerts, some of them are dead and nothing currently tells you which. Read-only over data that already exists.
