A score for how likely a builder is to be open to a conversation, inferred only from public signals: an explicit open-to-work marker, a bio change, a burst of side-project activity, a freshly rewritten profile README. It reuses the combined-signal-with-decay scoring already proven by the abuse risk score. This is the item that most needs care under our own privacy policy, so it ships with its inputs documented and visible rather than as an opaque number.
