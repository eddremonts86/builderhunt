Fifteen source adapters ship today. The next candidates are the places builders actually congregate that we do not read yet, chosen on the same rule as every existing one: a public, documented surface we can read without pretending to be a browser, and no scraping of anything behind a login. Suggestions in this direction are the most useful kind of roadmap vote.
