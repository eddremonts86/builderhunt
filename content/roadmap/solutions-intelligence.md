Advisory output that answers "should this be a person, an AI system, or a hybrid" for a given problem, with the evidence behind the recommendation. The product shell and its contracts exist; nothing behind them does. It stays planned rather than in progress because it depends on enrichment being live and on a graded quality bar being met before anyone should act on its advice.
