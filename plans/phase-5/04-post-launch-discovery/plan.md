# Post-launch discovery (plan)

## Order

1. **Recruit.** The screener and interview guide are built in phase 2 and ready to use; recruiting is the
   long pole and starts the day there are users to recruit from.
2. **Interview, one segment at a time**, in the order the product's own traffic justifies — `hiring`
   first, since that is the segment the current product serves and therefore the one with real users to
   ask.
3. **Synthesise**, then run the LLM critique on the anonymised findings — never on raw notes, and never
   as evidence in its own right.
4. **Decide the ICP**, and file the deltas against phase 2.

## What is already done, so nobody redoes it

Phase 2 keeps the parts an engineer or founder can finish without a participant:

- the interview guide, scorecard and recruiting screener, written so that no question presupposes
  BuilderHunt is the answer;
- the measurable baseline — signup, onboarding completion, first search, first saved builder, retention —
  with unmeasured metrics marked as unmeasured rather than estimated;
- the taxonomy itself, as a documented default with its reasoning.

## Verification

Nothing here is verified by a test. Two rules stand in for one:

- **No PII leaves the findings documents**, and no LLM output is presented as evidence. Both are
  properties of the artifact, checkable by reading it.
- **Every number cites its query, its time range and its source.** An estimate labelled as a measurement
  is the specific failure `project-hygiene` spent a whole plan removing from this repository, and research
  documents are not exempt from that standard.
